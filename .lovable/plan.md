# Nurses Connect homepage refresh

## What will change
- Restyle the existing homepage with a crisp blue, white, and soft green healthcare palette.
- Keep a polished responsive navigation with the Nurses Connect identity, requested links, Login, and Register.
- Replace the main heading and description with the exact supplied wording.
- Present “Find a Nurse” and “Join as a Nurse” as the two clear primary actions.
- Refine spacing, typography, image treatment, and mobile navigation for a professional result.

- Homepage only.
- Navigation items remain on-page placeholders; no other pages or account flows will be created.

## Technical details
- Update the existing React/Tailwind homepage and its semantic design tokens.
- Preserve accessibility, reduced-motion support, and mobile/desktop responsiveness.
- Verify the finished homepage in the live preview at desktop and mobile widths.
